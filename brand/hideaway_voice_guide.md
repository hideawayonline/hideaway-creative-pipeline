# Hideaway — Voice & Ad Reference

Condensed from *Hideaway Brand Guidelines 2026* + *BRAND Voice Bible*. This is the
copy/voice rule set the `/ad-replicate` pipeline follows when replicating and iterating
ads. **Visual specs live in `hideaway_brand_kit.json`.**

## Who we are (one line)
Fun, affordable self-care from the Gold Coast — designer-inspired fragrances and indulgent
body care that smell incredible and cost a fraction of the designer price.

## Archetype & personality
- **Jester × Lover** — playful, irreverent, bold colour + sensual, indulgent, emotional.
- Life of the party: playful, bold, cheeky, generous, joyful.
- **NOT:** pretentious, corporate, boring, exclusive, try-hard.

## Audience
Women 25–34 (reach 18–45), 80%+ female, Australia-wide (strong QLD/NSW/VIC). Coming from
designer fragrances (seeking value) or Bath & Body Works-type brands (seeking an upgrade).
"Believes she deserves luxury but isn't willing to be ripped off for it."
Personas: Fragrance Obsessive · Self-Care Ritualist · Gift Buyer · Self-Care Newbie.

## Voice attributes
- **Witty & clever** — puns, wordplay. "Smell like a million bucks for twenty."
- **Playful & fun** — "main character energy."
- **Warm & inclusive** — no gatekeeping. Everyone belongs.
- **Confidently casual** — talk like humans, contractions, conversational.

## Tone by channel (cheekiness / warmth)
| Channel | Cheekiness | Warmth |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / TikTok | HIGH | Medium |
| Facebook | Med–High | High |
| Ads (Performance / "ugly ads") | HIGH | Low–Med — punchy, benefit-led, raw/lo-fi |
| Ads (Brand) | Med–High | High — aspirational lifestyle |

## Writing rules
- **Australian English always** (colour, favourite, organise).
- Contractions encouraged. **Sentence case** headlines (not Title Case) — except product/brand names.
- **No Oxford comma.** Short paragraphs, punchy sentences.
- Emojis: yes on social/email, **no** on packaging/formal. Exclamation marks: max one per paragraph.
- Slang sparingly: "obsessed", "slay", "bestie", "main character energy".

## Words we LOVE / AVOID
- **Love:** indulge, treat yourself, obsessed, addicted, vibes, energy, joy, delight, magic,
  escape, holiday in a bottle, bougie on a budget, handcrafted, made with love.
- **Avoid:** leverage, synergy, utilise, purchase, enquire, furthermore, anti-aging, fix,
  flaws, "cheap/budget/discount" (use **affordable**), clients (use **customers/babes**),
  "as per our policy", "kind regards".

## Ad copy guidance
**Performance ("ugly ads"):** raw, lo-fi, scroll-stopping. Lead with bold claim/relatable hook.
Price comparison is powerful (**$20 vs $200**). UGC-style performs. Punchy, benefit-driven, strong CTA.
> "STOP scrolling if you love designer perfume but hate the price tag. This $20 perfume smells
> IDENTICAL to [Designer] ($189). Australian-made. Vegan. 4.8★ from 2,000+ reviews. Shop hideaway.online"

**Brand ads:** polished, aspirational, lifestyle-led, emotional hook — still fun, never stuffy.

## Taglines
- **Anchor:** Your passport to affordable luxury.
- **Performance:** Designer dupes that won't break the bank.
- **Positioning:** Luxury for everyone.
- **Campaign:** Why should luxury be boring?
- **Origin:** Made with love on the Gold Coast.

## Hero statements
- "Smell like a million bucks. Spend twenty."
- "Your daily escape starts here."
- "36+ scents. One obsession."
- "Self-care that actually feels like a treat."
- "Born on the Gold Coast. Made for your bathroom shelf."
- "Designer-inspired. Australian-made. Obsession-guaranteed."

## Proof points (use freely, truthfully)
Australian-made · handcrafted in Burleigh Heads · ~$20 vs $50+ designer · 36+ scents ·
vegan · cruelty-free · paraben-free · silicone-free · petroleum-free · 10+ year heritage.

### How the pipeline uses this
1. **Replicate:** OCR the reference ad's copy → keep the regional copy verbatim.
2. **Re-skin** the visual in the brand kit palette/aesthetic (Higgsfield).
3. **Iterate ×5:** vary ONE visual axis each (background · palette · layout · product framing ·
   CTA style). **Copy stays frozen.** If new copy is ever needed, it must follow every rule above.
4. **Voice-check** any generated copy against Words We Love/Avoid + writing rules before launch.
