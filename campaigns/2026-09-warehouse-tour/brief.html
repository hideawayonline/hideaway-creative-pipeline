Inside the warehouse

The warehouse tour reel is not behind-the-scenes content. It's the receipt for the letter we sent on 30 August — and it should be sent as one.

Send  Thu 11 Sep, 7:00pm AEST Audience  ~25,000 Source  38.2K views · 38 shares Status  Copy drafted, nothing scheduled

The read

The list already told us they were worried

On 30 August the ownership update went to the full file — 118,347 recipients. It opened at 57.7% against our usual 34–38%. People were paying close attention.

Two other numbers from that send tell the rest of the story.

MetricCEO letter, 30 AugTypical campaign
Recipients118,347~25,000
Open rate57.7%34–38%
Click-to-open0.17%~1.1%
Unsubscribe rate0.73%8×0.08–0.10%

Roughly 860 people unsubscribed off that one send.

Read together: a large part of the list opened because they were worried, read a claim, and got no proof. Some of them left.

A letter is a claim. A warehouse tour is evidence.

This is the follow-up chapter — the same message, shown instead of said. It's a trust email, not a promo. Revenue is the second-order effect, not the ask.

The framing

We told you we're here to stay. Here's the building.

That single line does three jobs at once: it closes the loop the letter opened for the people who wanted more than words, it re-warms the worried-but-still-subscribed before they churn too, and it earns an easy, low-commitment click.

One structural change

Host the tour on our own site, not Instagram

Click-to-open is sitting at roughly 1.1% across recent campaigns. People open and don't click. Sending them to Instagram makes that worse — the session ends on someone else's platform.

Build hideaway.online/pages/inside-the-warehouse with the reel embedded at the top, two or three lines underneath, and the range as a product grid below it. Same emotional payoff, but the click lands somewhere that can convert and we keep the traffic.

If the page can't be built in time, ship anyway — point the CTA at the collection and use the reel as a linked GIF. Don't hold the send for it.

Confirm before sending

Five assumptions in the draft

Each is a one-line fix if it's wrong.

  • Sign-offThe draft signs “Steve, Founder”. Match whichever name went on the 30 August letter, so the two read as one voice.
  • LocationCopy says “the Gold Coast”, which covers both the Robina address on the Klaviyo account and the “handmade in Burleigh Heads” line from the Delicate Rose launch. Swap in the suburb if you'd rather be precise.
  • “Everything you have ever ordered went out that door”True only if there's been no third-party fulfilment or site move. Soften to “Every order goes out that door” if the history is messier.
  • Video hostOn-site page or straight to the reel. On-site is the recommendation.
  • Hero stillPull a frame from the reel — the front-door shot with the “welcome to hideaway” sign is the strongest opener.
