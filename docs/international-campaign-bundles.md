## 3. Shipping economics (the whole game)

Australia Post retail international rates, 1 July 2026 (prepaid satchels; our contract/MyPost Business rates at 23K orders/quarter will be meaningfully cheaper — treat these as a conservative ceiling):

| Destination | 1kg | 2kg |
|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 — New Zealand | $29.00 | $43.80 |
| Zone 3 — USA/Canada | $43.60 | ~$66 (est.) |
| Zone 4 — UK/Europe | $49.90 | $78.50 |

**Two rules fall out of this:**
1. **Every bundle must pack under 1kg.** The 1kg→2kg step costs an extra $15–29. Design bundles at ≤850g product + ~120g packaging.
2. **One bundle per parcel.** Price so a single bundle covers its own freight; a second bundle in the same cart is nearly pure margin (2kg satchel is far less than 2× the 1kg rate) — so "add a second bundle, 15% off" is a strong AOV lever.

**A hidden 10% tailwind:** exports are GST-free. A $90 order shipped offshore nets the full $90, vs $81.82 ex-GST domestically. That ~$8 quietly funds a big chunk of the NZ postage on every order.

## 4. The hero offer — reviewed

> Proposal: **3 × Natural Lubricant + FREE Body Butter Oil — $90 AUD, free international shipping.**

Weight: 3×150g + 180g = 630g product, ~780g packed → **fits the 1kg band everywhere.** ✅

Unit economics per order (before product COGS and payment/ad costs):

| Market | Revenue | Postage (retail) | Left after postage |
|---|---|---|---|
| NZ | $90 | $29.00 | $61.00 |
| US | $90 | $43.60 | $46.40 |
| UK | $90 | $49.90 | $40.10 |

At a typical 20–30% COGS ($18–27 on $93 RRP of product), the bundle contributes roughly **$13–43 per order before ad spend** at retail postage — and more on contract rates. NZ is comfortably profitable; UK is the thin edge and should be priced/watched accordingly.

**One honest weakness:** the product value maths is soft. 3 lubes ($60) + oil ($33) = $93 RRP for $90 — a 3% discount. The *real* value is the $29–50 of freight we're absorbing, so the messaging must sell that, and the RRP anchor should be pushed over $100:

> **Recommended structure:** 3 × Natural Lubricant + FREE Body Butter Oil (worth $33) + FREE Mini Body Custard (worth $8) — **$90, shipped worldwide free.** "Over $100 of product + up to $50 of international freight on us. You pay what Aussies pay."

That's +$8 RRP and ~80g of weight (still ~860g packed, inside 1kg) for a dramatically better value story: **"$101 of product + $50 shipping = $150+ value for $90."**

**Competitive context (US):** Coconu oil-based and Woo "Coconut Love Oil" — the closest US natural/coconut lubricant comparables — retail around US$24–25 per bottle. Our 3-pack + free oil lands at ≈ **US$59**, i.e. three bottles for less than the price of 2.5 of theirs, gift included. The price story genuinely works in USD and GBP.

**Ad-policy caveat (important):** Meta restricts ads for products positioned around sexual pleasure; lubricant-led creative risks rejections/account flags. Run the lube bundle through **email, organic social, TikTok Shop and the landing page**, and let **paid ads lead with body-care bundles** (below) that click through to a page where Skin to Skin Worldwide is merchandised alongside.

## 6. Pricing & storefront mechanics

- **Shopify Markets** (already on Shopify Plus): enable NZ, US, UK, CA, IE, SG as active markets; present local currency converted from AUD at **0% price markup** — that IS the "Australian prices" promise. Show "Free international shipping" as the default rate for the bundle collection; exclude/blanket-block Perfume, Perfume (Travel), Body Mist product types from international markets via market catalogs.
- **Free-shipping threshold:** set international free shipping at **$90+** (i.e. any bundle qualifies; à-la-carte singles either unavailable internationally or charged real postage — this funnels everyone into bundles).
- **Duties & taxes:**
  - **US:** de minimis ended Aug 2025 — every parcel is now assessed duties. Cosmetics from AU carry the ~10% baseline tariff. Decide DDP (collect duties at checkout via Shopify Managed Markets — recommended, no doorstep surprise) vs DDU (cheaper sticker, angry customers). DDP is strongly recommended for the US.
  - **UK:** consignments ≤£135 require the seller to register and collect 20% UK VAT at checkout. Either register for UK VAT or launch UK via Managed Markets (merchant of record handles it).
  - **EU:** IOSS registration for ≤€150 consignments (phase 2+).
  - **NZ:** you're almost certainly past the NZD $60K threshold already at $263K/90d — confirm NZ GST registration status before scaling.
- **Regulatory (US) — flag for a 30-min legal check:** the US FDA classifies *personal lubricants* as Class II medical devices. Most indie brands route around this by marketing the product as an "intimate body oil / massage oil" (cosmetic). Recommend the US-market product copy uses "Intimate Body Oil" naming and avoids explicit lubricant claims until reviewed.
- **Shipping Protection:** keep the $3 attach for international; consider raising to $5 on international orders (loss/theft rates are higher, perceived value is higher).

## 7. Marketing plan

### Positioning
> **"Aussie prices. Worldwide. We pay the freight."**
> The cult Australian body-care brand — coconut-oil rituals, dessert-grade scents — now shipping to your door for exactly what Australians pay.

### Phased rollout

**Phase 0 — NZ scale-up (Weeks 1–2).** Cheapest postage ($29/1kg), existing demand, zero new compliance. Launch all 4 bundles to NZ; this is the live rehearsal for CVR, packaging survival and delivery SLA. Target: lift NZ AOV from $57 → $75+.

**Phase 1 — US + UK (Weeks 3–6).** After DDP/VAT decisions. English-language creative reuse; gourmand/dupe culture is at peak on US/UK TikTok. US is the volume prize (Coconu price comparison works hard here); UK is the margin-thin market — hold price at $90 but watch contribution.

**Phase 2 — CA, IE, SG (Weeks 7+).** Same Zone-3/2 economics, switch on per Markets config, no new creative needed.

### Channels

1. **Klaviyo (week 1, near-zero cost):**
   - Segments: existing NZ customers; any non-AU/NZ profiles (email captures from blocked checkouts, historical intl browsers).
   - "We now ship to you" announcement flow (3 emails: announcement → value math/social proof → 48h mini-gift urgency).
   - Geo-branch the abandoned-cart flow: international carts get a "yes, shipping really is free — here's how" reassurance email.
   - Post-purchase international flow: dispatch expectations, customs FAQ (US), melt-care card content.
2. **Meta ads:** lead with **The Glow Ritual** (fully policy-safe). Creative angles: (a) price-shock split screen "their one bottle vs our whole ritual", (b) "Australians have been gatekeeping this" UGC, (c) the value-math receipt graphic ($105 product + $50 freight = $90). 18+ targeting, NZ lookalikes seeded from 3,628 existing NZ buyers, then US/UK broad.
3. **TikTok organic + Shop:** unboxings, "restock my shower" ASMR, couples' content for Skin to Skin (organic tolerates what paid doesn't). The existing `pipeline.py` creative-generation pipeline can be pointed at international hooks (add market-specific hook lines + currency overlays).
4. **Influencer seeding:** 30 micro-creators (10 NZ / 10 US / 10 UK) get the hero bundle; brief them on the "Aussie prices worldwide" line and a market-specific code (tracks by geo).
5. **Landing page:** dedicated `/worldwide` collection page — shipping-promise banner, delivery time by country, duties-included badge (US), bundle builder embed, Coconu/Woo price-comparison module for US visitors.

### Budget guide (first 6 weeks)
- 60% Meta (Glow Ritual, NZ→US/UK), 20% TikTok Spark, 10% influencer seeding, 10% testing reserve.
- Gate: each market must hit **contribution ≥ $20/order after postage + COGS + ads** by end of week 4 or price/offer gets restructured before more spend.
