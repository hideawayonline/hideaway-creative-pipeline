# hideaway — Perfume Bottle Design Brief

_Created 2026-06-15. Design exploration → US dupe-market research → final concepts for the top 3 best-selling scents._

All concept renders are hosted on the image CDN (links below). They are also posted to the team Slack (`#team-creativeinspo`) and DM'd to Steven Evans.

## 1. Decision summary

- **Chosen bottle silhouette:** the **chunky colour-block** shape (Saltair-style) — a heavy rounded-rectangle in weighted glass with a thick, oversized glossy colour-block cap.
- **House system:** keep the same hardware across the whole range (weighted glass body + thick colour-block cap + lowercase `hideaway.` wordmark with the pink full stop). Each scent gets its own **body + cap colour pairing** for instant shelf/feed recognition.
- **Why this shape:** bold, playful, very photogenic ("shelfie"/TikTok-friendly), reads premium thanks to glass weight + substantial cap, and is a distinct, ownable house trade dress (important — see legal note).

## 3. US dupe-market research — bottle & packaging takeaways

Focus: bottle & packaging design in the US affordable/"dupe" fragrance market. (Sources gathered via web research; brand-site specifics came from search snippets as most sites block automated fetch. Quantitative stats from supplier blogs are directional.)

**Three winning looks:**
- **Clinical-minimalist "inspired by"** — Dossier: uniform clear heavy glass, white label printing scent name + notes, magnetic metal cap, pale-pink carton with a notes/inspiration card. 50 ml ~$39.
- **Scent-led colour-coded** — Oakcha: colour-coated heavy glass (coating = UV protection), soft-touch finish, magnetic cap, stainless-steel atomizer, magnetic-closure box. 30/50 ml, ~30% extrait, ~$35–60.
- **Ornate "affordable luxury"** — Lattafa/Arabian houses: sculpted gold caps, embossing, heavy glass, collectible. (This is the direction we rejected for hideaway.)

**Premium-but-affordable signal levers (highest → lower confidence):**
1. **Weight** — thick glass base + weighted metal (Zamac) cap. The single most consistent value cue. (Surlyn caps = glass-like heft without metal cost.)
2. Magnetic cap; foil stamping / embossing; soft-touch lamination; magnetic-closure rigid box.
3. Restrained or scent-led colour; generous negative space; bold/uppercase typography.
- **Cheap tells:** thin light glass, loose/crooked caps, sputtering sprayers, pixelated/misaligned labels, paper stickers.

**Trends performing on TikTok/Amazon:** frosted glass (photogenic + UV protection), bold legible typography, scent-led colour, gourmand/"lickable" (vanilla, coconut top sellers), unboxing-as-marketing (magnetic "click").

**Formats & pricing (USD):** core 50 ml ~$39 (Dossier) / 30–50 ml extrait ~$35–60 (Oakcha); travel 10–15 ml; discovery sets = 2 ml vials bundled with a credit toward a full bottle (acquisition funnel); bundles like ALT "3 for $99 / 5 for $149."

## 4. Legal note (not legal advice — get IP review before tooling)

- **"Inspired by" / "our impression of" naming is defensible in the US** (Smith v. Chanel, 9th Cir. 1968 — you may name the original you copied in truthful comparative advertising).
- **The risk is trade dress, not the scent reference.** Do **not** copy a specific brand's bottle silhouette, cap, colour-coding, label layout, or signature font.
- ⚠️ **Sol de Janeiro is currently suing the Australian dupe brand MCoBeauty** (S.D.N.Y., 2025) over trade dress described as "transparent bottle, bold colour-coded labeling, rounded metallic/pearlescent cap, bold sans-serif font displaying the scent number and name." An Australian brand entering the US should keep a **distinct hideaway house look** rather than mirror any one competitor.
- **FDA basics:** identity, net contents, manufacturer/distributor, INCI ingredient list ("fragrance" permitted), country of origin; MoCRA is expanding fragrance-allergen disclosure.
